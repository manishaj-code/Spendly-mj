Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service: authenticating your account, saving and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and category breakdowns for you.

We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and email delivery services) to operate the Service. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
